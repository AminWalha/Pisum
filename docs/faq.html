Diagnostic Assistance & AI Intelligence
PISUM is an advanced diagnostic assistance and clinical reporting application built specifically for practicing radiologists. Its primary mission is to act as a second reader and diagnostic accelerator: it analyzes raw observations, cross-correlates imaging signs to detect unified syndromes, calibrates diagnostic certainty with academic rigor, and structures complex findings into standardized templates.

While traditional tools focus only on speech recognition, PISUM focuses on clinical synthesis — elevating preliminary notes into publication-grade conclusions while prioritizing critical findings.
Instead of simply formatting text, PISUM evaluates the clinical conjunction of multiple individual imaging findings. When you describe findings such as hepatic vein occlusion, caudate lobe enlargement, and ascites, PISUM identifies the unifying entity (e.g. Budd-Chiari Syndrome) rather than leaving isolated observations.

It automatically structures the conclusion according to 4 objective diagnostic certainty tiers:
  • Confirming: Direct, unambiguous hallmark signs.
  • Suggestive of: Strong pattern matching with known clinical correlation.
  • Compatible with: Consistent pattern, non-specific differential possible.
  • Cannot exclude: Subtle or indirect imaging markers requiring vigilance.
PISUM uses an Emergency-First architecture. Life-threatening conditions (such as aortic dissections, acute pulmonary embolisms, tension pneumothoraces, intracranial herniations, or active retroperitoneal bleeding) are automatically detected and pinned to the very first bullet point of the report's Impression.

Critical alerts are tagged with an urgency notification to ensure life-saving information is never buried under routine anatomical boilerplate.

Reporting, Templates & Dictation Tools
Yes. While diagnostic synthesis is its core strength, PISUM integrates Sally AI, a real-time medical voice recognition engine. You can dictate findings conversationally or in short telegraphic phrases across 23 languages.

The diagnostic engine immediately ingests this spoken input, parses relevant anatomy, corrects medical phrasing, and directs each observation into the appropriate structured section of the report.
PISUM includes 112 expert-designed structured templates covering all imaging modalities: CT, MRI, X-Ray, Ultrasound, and PET-CT across every organ system (neuroradiology, musculoskeletal, chest, abdomen, pelvis, cardiovascular).

You can also customize existing templates or create unlimited custom templates from scratch to match your subspecialty conventions and institutional standards.
Yes. With built-in medical translation, any completed radiology report can be converted in one click into any of 23 supported languages (including French, English, German, Spanish, Arabic, Italian, and Portuguese).

Unlike generic translators, PISUM preserves specialized radiological nomenclature, ACR/BI-RADS classifications, and anatomical definitions while creating a distinct translated copy that leaves the original document untouched.
Reports export in one click to PDF and native Microsoft Word (.docx), complete with clinical letterheads, custom headers, footers, logos, and digital signatures.

On Expert and Clinic plans, PISUM supports LAN Network Sync: all reading workstations in your department or practice can share the local patient database and exam queue over an internal folder without requiring a complex cloud infrastructure.

Privacy, Security & Compliance
Patient confidentiality is protected by design: all patient reports, patient identifiers, and database records remain encrypted locally on your workstation (AES-256). Clinical information is never permanently stored on external cloud databases.

AI processing queries are anonymized, handled in memory via secure APIs, and audio or patient records are never retained or used to train third-party machine learning models. User license and authentication data is hosted on ISO 27001-certified European servers (Supabase EU).
